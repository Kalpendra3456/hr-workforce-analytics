# HR Workforce Analytics

## Project Overview

### Business Background

Human Resources is one of the most important departments in every organization. HR teams manage recruitment, employee performance, retention, promotions, salaries, training, and workforce planning. As organizations grow, employee data becomes larger and more complex, making it difficult to identify trends through manual reporting.

This project transforms raw HR data into meaningful business insights using SQL, Python, and Power BI. It simulates how a Business Intelligence or Data Analytics team supports HR leadership with data-driven decision making.

---

## Business Problem

HR managers often struggle to answer critical business questions such as:

- Which departments have the highest employee turnover?
- Are employees leaving because of low salaries?
- Which teams have poor performance ratings?
- Is workforce diversity improving?
- Which departments require immediate hiring?
- Are training programs improving employee performance?
- Which locations have the highest attrition rates?

Without proper analytics, organizations risk increasing hiring costs, reduced employee satisfaction, lower productivity, and poor workforce planning.

---

## Project Goal

The primary objective of this project is to build a complete HR Workforce Analytics solution that converts HR operational data into actionable business intelligence.

The project demonstrates how organizations can monitor workforce health, identify employee trends, optimize HR strategies, and support executive decision-making.
