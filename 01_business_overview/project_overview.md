# Project Overview

## Project Name

**HR Workforce Analytics**

---

# Introduction

HR Workforce Analytics is an end-to-end business intelligence project designed to analyze employee data and transform raw HR information into meaningful business insights.

Organizations generate large volumes of employee data across recruitment, workforce management, compensation, performance, promotions, training, and employee exits. Without analytics, HR teams struggle to identify trends, monitor workforce health, and make strategic decisions.

This project demonstrates how modern Data Analytics can help Human Resource departments improve operational efficiency through SQL analysis, Python-based data engineering, and interactive Power BI dashboards.

---

# Business Context

Human Resource departments are responsible for attracting, developing, retaining, and managing organizational talent. As organizations expand, employee information becomes increasingly difficult to analyze using spreadsheets or manual reporting.

Business leaders require accurate, real-time workforce insights to answer questions such as:

- Is the workforce growing sustainably?
- Which departments have high employee turnover?
- Are salaries competitive across departments?
- Which teams require recruitment?
- How effective are employee training programs?
- Which employees are top performers?
- Is workforce diversity improving over time?

This project provides analytical solutions to these business challenges.
