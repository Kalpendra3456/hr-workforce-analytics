# KPI Dictionary

## Overview

This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) used throughout the HR Workforce Analytics project. These KPIs provide standardized business metrics that help HR managers and executives monitor workforce health, employee performance, recruitment effectiveness, and organizational growth.

---

# Workforce KPIs

| KPI | Business Definition | Business Purpose |
|------|---------------------|------------------|
| Total Employees | Total number of employees in the organization | Measure workforce size |
| Active Employees | Employees currently employed | Monitor current workforce |
| Terminated Employees | Employees who have left the organization | Track workforce exits |
| Workforce Growth | Net increase in employees over time | Measure organizational growth |
| Average Employee Age | Average age of all employees | Workforce planning |
| Average Experience | Average years of employee experience | Talent assessment |
| Average Tenure | Average duration employees stay in the company | Employee retention analysis |

---

# Recruitment KPIs

| KPI | Business Definition | Business Purpose |
|------|---------------------|------------------|
| Total Hires | Total employees hired during a period | Recruitment tracking |
| Hiring Rate | Percentage increase in workforce through hiring | Recruitment performance |
| Recruitment Source Efficiency | Number of hires by recruitment source | Optimize hiring channels |
| Time to Hire | Average hiring duration | Improve recruitment process |
| New Hire Percentage | Percentage of newly hired employees | Workforce expansion monitoring |

# Attrition KPIs

| KPI | Business Definition | Business Purpose |
|------|---------------------|------------------|
| Attrition Rate | Percentage of employees leaving the organization | Employee retention monitoring |
| Voluntary Attrition | Employees leaving voluntarily | Identify resignation trends |
| Involuntary Attrition | Employees terminated by the company | HR policy evaluation |
| Department Attrition | Attrition rate within each department | Identify high-risk departments |
| Retention Rate | Percentage of employees retained | Measure workforce stability |

# Business Value

These KPIs enable HR leaders to:

- Monitor workforce health
- Improve employee retention
- Optimize recruitment strategies
- Evaluate employee performance
- Support compensation planning
- Improve workforce diversity
- Enhance talent development
- Monitor organizational growth
- Enable data-driven HR decision-making
- Build executive HR dashboards with meaningful business metrics
