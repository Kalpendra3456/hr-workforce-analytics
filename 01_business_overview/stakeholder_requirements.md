# Stakeholder Requirements

## Purpose

This document outlines the business requirements, expectations, and reporting needs of key stakeholders involved in the HR Workforce Analytics project. The objective is to ensure that the analytics solution delivers actionable insights that support operational and strategic decision-making.

---

# Project Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Business Responsibility |
|--------------|----------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) | Executive Leadership | Overall HR strategy and workforce planning |
| HR Director | Department Head | Monitor HR performance and workforce metrics |
| HR Business Partner | Business Support | Support department-level HR decisions |
| Talent Acquisition Manager | Recruitment | Hiring strategy and recruitment performance |
| Learning & Development Manager | Employee Development | Training and skill development |
| Compensation & Benefits Manager | Payroll | Salary planning and compensation analysis |
| Department Managers | Operations | Team performance and employee management |
| Executive Leadership | Business Decision Makers | Strategic workforce planning |

---

# Business Requirements

## Workforce Overview

The HR leadership team requires:

- Total employee headcount
- Active and inactive employee count
- Workforce distribution by department
- Workforce distribution by location
- Employee growth trends
- Organization-wide workforce summary

---

## Recruitment Requirements

HR recruitment teams need to monitor:

- Total hires
- Hiring trends by month and year
- Hiring by department
- Hiring by location
- Recruitment source effectiveness
- Departments with hiring shortages

---

## Employee Demographics

Stakeholders require visibility into:

- Gender distribution
- Age distribution
- Education levels
- Marital status
- Ethnicity distribution
- Employee location analysis

---

## Attrition Monitoring

Management requires dashboards showing:

- Overall attrition rate
- Department-wise attrition
- Job role attrition
- Location-wise turnover
- Voluntary resignations
- High-risk employee groups

---

## Compensation Analysis

HR leadership requires:

- Average salary
- Salary distribution
- Payroll cost by department
- Salary comparison across locations
- Salary by experience level
- High and low salary bands

---

## Performance Management

Managers require:

- Employee performance ratings
- Department performance comparison
- Top-performing employees
- Low-performing employees
- Manager performance summary
- Performance improvement opportunities

---

## Promotion Analytics

HR requires reports on:

- Promotion rates
- Promotion trends
- Time to promotion
- Promotions by department
- High-potential employees

---

## Training Analytics

Learning & Development teams require:

- Training participation
- Training completion rate
- Certification completion
- Training effectiveness
- Employees requiring additional training

---

## Executive Dashboard Requirements

The executive dashboard should display:

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Hiring Rate
- Average Salary
- Average Employee Age
- Average Tenure
- Average Performance Rating
- Promotion Rate
- Training Completion Rate
- Gender Diversity Ratio
- Department-wise Headcount

---

# Functional Requirements

The analytics solution should:

- Clean and transform raw HR data
- Calculate business KPIs
- Support SQL-based business analysis
- Generate executive dashboards in Power BI
- Provide interactive filtering by department, location, job role, and time period
- Enable drill-down analysis for detailed workforce insights

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

The solution should:

- Ensure data accuracy and consistency
- Handle large HR datasets efficiently
- Be scalable for future data additions
- Maintain clear documentation
- Produce reports that are easy for business users to interpret
- Support reusable analytics workflows

---

# Expected Business Outcomes

The HR Workforce Analytics solution should enable stakeholders to:

- Improve workforce planning
- Reduce employee attrition
- Optimize recruitment strategies
- Monitor workforce diversity
- Improve employee performance
- Support fair compensation decisions
- Increase training effectiveness
- Enhance executive decision-making through data-driven insights

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if stakeholders can:

- Access reliable workforce KPIs
- Identify workforce trends quickly
- Monitor HR performance in real time
- Make informed strategic decisions
- Reduce manual reporting efforts
- Improve overall HR operational efficiency
