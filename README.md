# HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Workforce Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to help HR leaders monitor workforce performance, employee costs, departmental profitability, and workforce distribution. The dashboard transforms raw HR data into actionable business insights through dynamic KPIs, charts, and drill-down analysis.

The report is built with a multi-page navigation system, allowing users to switch between different analytical views without leaving the dashboard.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The primary goal of this dashboard is to help HR and business stakeholders:

* Monitor workforce performance across departments.
* Analyze employee salary and labor costs.
* Identify the most profitable departments.
* Understand employee distribution across cities.
* Track organizational budget utilization.
* Support data-driven workforce planning.

# 📊 Key Insights Found

### Revenue

* Total Net Revenue reached **2.62 Billion**.
* Finance department generated one of the highest gross revenues.
* Revenue is distributed fairly evenly across major departments.

### Profitability

* Total organizational profit is **980 Million**.
* Marketing contributes the highest departmental profit.
* Some departments generate similar revenue but lower profitability due to higher operational costs.

### Labor Cost

* Total labor cost is **324 Million**.
* Salary spending remains relatively consistent across departments.
* High salary expenses do not always translate into higher profits.

### Workforce

* Organization has **53 employees** in the dataset.
* Employees are spread across multiple cities.
* Bengaluru contains one of the largest employee populations.

### Salary Analysis

* Maximum salary is approximately **284M**.
* Minimum salary is approximately **81M**.
* Large salary variation suggests opportunities for compensation review.

### Employee Performance

* Employee performance and goal completion can be tracked individually.
* Managers can identify high-performing employees for recognition.
* Low-performing employees can be targeted for training.
